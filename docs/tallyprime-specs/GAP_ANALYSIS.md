## 1. Executive Summary of Audited Baseline vs. Identified Discrepancies

### 1.1 Modules Already at 100% Parity (`₹ 0.00` Variance Verified)
- **Core Accounting, 28 Account Groups, 24 Voucher Types & Double-Entry Engine**: `mc_journal` / `mc_entry` deferred constraint trigger `trg_mc_journal_double_entry_balance`, `ExactMoneyValidator` (`BigDecimal` / `ExactDecimal`), Bill-Wise (`NEW_REF`, `AGST_REF`, `ADVANCE`, `ON_ACCOUNT`), Cost Centres, Multi-Currency (`Unadjusted Forex Gain/Loss`), and MCA Edit Log (`mc_edit_log_version` + `MC-AUDIT-2`).
- **Inventory & Valuation Engine**: Hierarchical Stock Groups, Categories, UOMs, Godowns, Batches/Expiry, Price Lists, BOM Manufacturing Journals, and back-dated `FIFO` (`₹ 1,340.00`) vs high-precision `Avg. Cost` (`₹ 1,143.04`) valuation (`Golden Vector B`).
- **Statutory GST, IMS, GSTR-9 & TDS**: Multi-GSTIN, 6-tier GST rate inheritance, RCM, Rel 7.2 Tobacco Deemed MRP, `GSTR-1`, `GSTR-2B`, `IMS` (with Partial ITC Reduction & Remarks), `GSTR-3B`, One-Click `GSTR-9`, 30-day e-Invoice/e-Way Bill session SPI, and Protean FVU 1.2 `Form 26Q`.
- **Dynamic `F11` (28 Features), Context-Sensitive `F12` & Keyboard Shortcuts**: In-place `F11` (`Show More` / `Show All`), context-sensitive `F12` (`Voucher`, `Master`, `Report`, `Print`), `Show Inactive` one-click feature activation, and `Alt+G` non-destructive modal stack.
